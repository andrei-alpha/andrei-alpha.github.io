Material Science

The Woods of This Build

Combining complementary wood species requires understanding density, shrinkage coefficients, and cellular structure under knife impact.

European Beech

Fagus sylvatica

Native to Central Europe and Switzerland. Beech is renowned in traditional woodworking and food preparation for its neutral scent, tight grain, and natural antibacterial qualities. When treated with mineral oil, it deepens into a warm, creamy golden amber.

Janka Hardness
1,450 lbf (Medium-Hard)
Grain Character
Uniform, fine pores
Origin
Central Europe / Swiss Jura
Culinary Safety
Exemplary food rating

In this board: Forms the bright, light-reflective tiles of the checkerboard matrix, providing structural toughness and easy knife glide.

Purpleheart (Amaranth)

Peltogyne spp.

An extraordinarily dense tropical hardwood. When freshly cut in the shop, it appears a muted dull grayish-brown. Exposure to UV light and air triggers natural photochemical oxidation, blooming into a breathtaking, royal magenta-violet that intensifies upon oiling.

Janka Hardness
2,520 lbf (Ultra-Dense)
Grain Character
Straight to interlocked
Color Shift
Oxidizes to vivid purple
Durability
Extreme rot resistance

In this board: Provides deep chromatic drama and exceptional wear resistance along knife paths.

Workshop Techniques

The Four Pillars of Precision

Why geometrical end-grain boards are both a technical challenge and a culinary heirloom.

01

End-Grain Kindness

Unlike edge-grain boards where blades sever wood fibers, end-grain boards orient fibers vertically like the bristles of a brush. The blade slips between fibers, self-healing over time and keeping kitchen knives razor sharp.

02

The 45° Clamping Challenge

Clamping diamonds diagonally induces lateral hydraulic shear: wet glue acts as a lubricant, causing rows to slip out of square. We combat this using staged sub-assemblies and custom melamine cauls.

03

Sub-Millimeter Tolerances

Every single square block must be cross-cut with zero tolerance variation. Even a 0.2mm error across an 8-block run cascades into a 1.6mm misalignment, ruining the checkerboard crispness.

04

Food-Safe Oil Satiation

Wood end-grain acts like vertical drinking straws. We submerge the finished piece in pure USP mineral oil until fully saturated, sealed with warm organic beeswax board butter for water repellency.
